## More AC keywords worth targeting next

These are common searches in Indian cities. Add a page or a section only for services you actually offer.

- **Brand searches:** "daikin ac service vadodara", "voltas ac repair vadodara", "lg ac service vadodara", "samsung ac repair vadodara", "blue star ac service vadodara". A short page per brand you service a lot can rank well.
- **Cleaning types:** "ac jet pump service", "ac foam cleaning", "ac deep cleaning vadodara". Only if you do jet or foam cleaning.
- **Price searches:** "ac service charges vadodara", "ac gas filling price". The pages already show prices; keep them current.
- **Problem searches:** "ac not cooling", "ac water leaking", "ac making noise", "ac error code E1/E4/F1". Blog-style help articles bring visitors who then call.
- **Area + service:** "ac service alkapuri", "ac repair gotri", "ac service manjalpur". Don't make 12 copy-paste area pages (Google treats those as spam). Mention real jobs in those areas in Google Business posts instead.

## What will move rankings the most (in order)

1. **Google Business Profile** (google.com/business). Most "ac service vadodara" searches on phones show the Google Maps box first, above all websites. Create or claim the profile for Relax Aircon, category "Air conditioning repair service", add phone, hours, service areas, photos of real jobs, and the website link.
2. **Reviews.** After every job, send customers your Google review link on WhatsApp. Reviews are the biggest factor for the Maps box.
3. **Listings.** Add the business to Justdial, Sulekha, IndiaMART and Facebook with exactly the same name, phone and address. Each one links back to the site.
4. **Real photos.** Before/after photos of jobs on the site and on Google Business.
5. **Patience.** New pages usually take 2 to 8 weeks to start ranking. Check Search Console → Performance monthly.
